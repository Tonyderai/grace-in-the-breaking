Where Broken Becomes Beautiful

Sometimes life circumstances break us into a million pieces — and at times, shattered to dust. We have all been there.

I thank God for handling me with grace in my seasons of brokenness, so I can be a testimony of His Amazing Grace. That is the essence of this space: to share what God has taught me and others in our seasons of brokenness.

My prayer is that you will glean from these writings, be enlarged, and be empowered to live powerful lives of purpose — as God intended.

Here you will find words of encouragement and testimonies — not only from me, but also from my Queens — women who have thrived through life's storms by the grace of God.

— Trudy
